## Payout, as a proxy

Total functional expenses ran 11.2% of end-of-year assets on average. This is a proxy, not the 990-PF qualifying-distribution figure, it counts overhead alongside grants and misses distributions counted elsewhere. It answers "is this funder spending like an operator or holding like an endowment," and nothing finer than that.
