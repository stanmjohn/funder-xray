Grants paid is contributions, gifts, grants paid, from 990-PF Part I line 25, column (a). Charitable disbursements are total expenses for exempt purposes, from Part I line 26, column (d), which adds the foundation's own charitable spending to its grants. The fair market value of assets is the end-of-year figure from item I in the form's header. The field meanings come from the IRS field guide linked on the [IRS annual extract page](https://www.irs.gov/statistics/soi-tax-stats-annual-extract-of-tax-exempt-organization-financial-data). The required payout is the distributable amount, from Part X line 7, which the IRS defines as the minimum payout a private foundation must distribute. It starts from 5 percent of the value of assets not used for charitable purposes. The IRS defines these terms on its [private foundations terms page](https://www.irs.gov/statistics/soi-tax-stats-private-foundations-study-terms-and-concepts). Charitable disbursements are close to the qualifying distributions the IRS counts against the required payout, but they are not the same figure. Treat a No as a reason to open the 990-PF, not as a finding.

## What this report refuses to do

That needs every year's e-file read the same way, which is a separate build. It also draws no conclusion a linked filing cannot support.
